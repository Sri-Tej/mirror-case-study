This repository examines the Deutsche Bank "mirror trades" case, in which clients bought Russian equities for rubles in Moscow and a related counterparty sold identical securities for US dollars in London. The offsetting trades carried no real economic risk but moved roughly $10 billion out of Russia. The case study covers the mechanics of the scheme, the failures in Deutsche Bank's anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer controls, and the 2017 penalties from the UK FCA and the New York DFS. It is intended for compliance, risk, and financial-crime education.
